Islam & My Faith

I am Muslim. I identify with the Athari creed, al-ʿAqīdah al-Athariyyah, and strive to follow the Salaf al-Ṣāliḥ as closely as possible.

Tawḥīd

التَّوْحِيد

Tawḥīd, the affirmation of Allah’s oneness and His exclusive right to worship, is at the heart of Islam. The short sūrah al-Ikhlāṣ is a starting point for reading how the Qur’an expresses divine oneness.

The early generations

Salaf al-Ṣāliḥ means the righteous predecessors. The earliest Muslim generations include the Ṣaḥābah, the Companions of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, and the Tābiʿūn, who learned from the Companions. Their place in the transmission of knowledge informs the way I approach Islamic history and religious study.

The study of transmitted knowledge

Hadith collections, classical works of creed, scholarly biographies and commentaries preserve different aspects of the Islamic scholarly tradition. Comparing a manuscript page with an edition opens questions about transmission, scribes, and how the book was read across generations.
