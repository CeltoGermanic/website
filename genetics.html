I use population genetics to ask questions about demographic history and to give family research a broader context. Genetic similarity, genealogical descent and cultural identity answer different questions.

G25, PCA and Vahaduo

PCA summarizes patterns of variation in a set of data. G25 coordinates provide a 25-dimensional representation used in hobbyist comparisons. A two-axis view can hide differences present on other axes. Vahaduo can compare distances and fit reference mixtures in coordinate space. Distances and model weights depend on the references, coordinate scaling and the model used.

A synthetic reference is a calculated average or weighted combination of other references. It can help explore a hypothesis. Its apparent fit does not establish that a discrete historical population with that exact profile existed.

Ancient Iberia in the published record

Olalde and colleagues reported genome-wide data from 271 ancient Iberians across six periods. Select a period to compare coverage and read its context. These are study sample counts.

Samples per period · Olalde et al. (2019) · n = 271

  • The smallest period group in this study. That scarcity limits comparisons within the period.

  • The study traces the arrival of Anatolian farmer-related ancestry and later changes involving hunter-gatherer-related ancestry.

  • These samples help set a baseline before ancestry related to steppe populations became widespread in Iberia.

  • The authors report major changes in genome-wide ancestry and in paternal lineages after roughly 2000 BCE.

  • Sampling spans groups with different languages, complicating efforts to equate language with one genetic history.

  • The largest period group. Later samples illuminate gene flow involving the Mediterranean and North Africa.

Across Iberia and northern Europe

Iberian research and later work on batch effects show why fine-scale similarities need independent checks. For the Azores, island-level samples and settlement histories matter. A pooled Portuguese reference may obscure differences among islands or between an island and the mainland.

Germanic and Flemish population history both intersect with migration, neighbouring communities and regional continuity. Linguistic and historical labels have a different scope from reference sets. Y-DNA describes one direct paternal line, with a much narrower scope than autosomal data.

Indigenous ancestry and archaeogenetics

My Charrúa connection comes from family history. Genetic ancestry estimates are a distinct kind of evidence. The ancient genomes studied in Uruguay by Lindo and colleagues document two individuals from one site and raise questions about earlier demographic movements. They cannot stand in for an entire Indigenous people or establish a family relationship.
