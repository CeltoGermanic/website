  1. Heritage

    Five histories meet in my family. They extend from the Azores and the northwest Iberian borderlands to the North Sea, Santa Catarina and the Río de la Plata.

  2. Azorean Heritage

    My maternal Portuguese ancestry is predominantly Azorean. The history of Desterro, now Florianópolis, offers regional context for that heritage.

  3. Galician Heritage

    Galicia is part of my maternal family history and a strong cultural and historical interest. The surviving details of that family connection are limited.

  4. The Weber Line

    Weber is my paternal family surname. The history of German-speaking regions in Europe and southern Brazil gives context to this heritage.

  5. Flemish Heritage

    Flemish-Germanic heritage is part of my paternal family history. Flanders has a distinct history in the Low Countries.

  6. My Charrúa Heritage

    Charrúa heritage is part of my family memory. I explore that connection through the history of the Río de la Plata and the voices of Charrúa descendants.

  7. Genealogy

    Weber is the family surname shared in this archive. This page brings together broad heritage branches and historical events that help place them in context.

  8. About

    I’m Brazilian and Muslim. This site brings together the family histories I inherited, the faith I practice and the subjects I keep returning to.

  9. Islam & My Faith

    I am Muslim. I identify with the Athari creed, al-ʿAqīdah al-Athariyyah, and strive to follow the Salaf al-Ṣāliḥ as closely as possible.

  10. The Athari Creed

    Al-ʿAqīdah al-Athariyyah names the creed I identify with. Its emphasis on revelation and transmitted reports shapes how I approach learning.

  11. Early Islamic History

    I’m interested in the relationship between the early Muslim community, the changing political world of the caliphates, and the preservation of religious knowledge.

  12. Classical Scholarship

    A reading room for Arabic religious literature, the sciences of hadith and the manuscripts that preserve them.

  13. Historical Interests

    I’m drawn to the way maps, archaeology, books and administrative records preserve different accounts of the same places.

  14. Population Genetics

    I use population genetics to ask questions about demographic history and to give family research a broader context. Genetic similarity, genealogical descent and cultural identity answer different questions.

  15. Languages & Linguistics

    Portuguese and English are part of the linguistic landscape of this site. Arabic, Galician, Gothic and the historical Germanic languages are particular subjects of interest.

  16. Technology & Computing

    I’m interested in how things work: how browsers render a page, how local models fit into memory, and how a game’s feel depends on the machine running it.

  17. Gaming & Digital Culture

    Counter-Strike 2’s HvH scene and Far Cry 3’s island atmosphere are two very different reasons games belong in this archive.
