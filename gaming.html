Gaming & Digital Culture

Counter-Strike 2 · Hack vs Hack

My main interest in CS2 is HvH, or Hack vs Hack. It is a subculture of custom environments where participants expect modified play. Its communities build a shared vocabulary around configurations, rules, server styles, map habits and the way different updates change the scene.

Configuration culture includes the sharing and comparison of presets and preferences. The technical questions I follow include how client and server state diverge, how tick timing and latency affect what players observe, and how custom server rules reshape movement and positioning. Map geometry, peeking and round pacing remain part of how players read a match. These interests sit alongside ordinary Counter-Strike customization, including HUDs, visual settings and binds.

Far Cry 3 · The Rook Islands

Tropical shorelines, dense vegetation and steep interior ridges make exploration central to the game’s atmosphere. The contrast between a clear coastal horizon and short sightlines under the canopy makes moving around the fictional Rook Islands its own pleasure.
