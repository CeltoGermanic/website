A personal introduction

About

I’m Brazilian and Muslim. This site brings together the family histories I inherited, the faith I practice and the subjects I keep returning to.

Family and place

Santa Catarina is an important point of reference in my family history. My mother’s side carries predominantly Azorean Portuguese ancestry and a Galician connection. The Weber surname belongs to my paternal line, with Germanic and Flemish heritage. My Charrúa heritage also has a place of its own in this archive.

Faith and study

Islam is central to my life. I identify with the Athari creed and strive to follow the Salaf al-Ṣāliḥ. Studying the early generations, Arabic religious terms and the ways narrations were transmitted helps me understand the tradition I seek to follow.

Other things I return to

I’m drawn to historical maps, population genetics, languages and the practical side of computing. Old manuscripts sit comfortably in this collection alongside local AI and games. Counter-Strike 2’s HvH scene and the tropical exploration of Far Cry 3 are very different sides of my interest in digital culture.
