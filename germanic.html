The Weber Line

Weber is my paternal family surname. The history of German-speaking regions in Europe and southern Brazil gives context to this heritage.

Southwestern German territories

The Odenwald is an example of the changing historical geography of southwestern Germany. Regions, states and church jurisdictions evolved over time, so the same place can appear under different administrative names in historical sources.

The Weser and northern trade

The Weser basin and ports such as Bremen are useful for studying trade and settlement in northern German territories. River transport connected inland settlements to North Sea commerce. Hanover and Lower Saxony offer other points of reference for the region’s political history.

Santa Catarina

German-speaking communities form a distinct part of Santa Catarina’s immigration history. Language, community institutions and historical archives provide ways to study how settlers and their descendants adapted to southern Brazil.
